The engineering problem

Limit the devices, keep playback tied to one.

IPTV needed to limit how many devices can watch at once, and to keep playback tied to the device that logged in, while traffic grew and a zone could fail.

Decisions and trade-offs

DECISION 1

Sessions tied to a device

A login creates a session. The token is opaque and bound to that device, and playback checks it while the user watches.

DECISION 2

An atomic limit

The concurrent-device limit is configurable and enforced atomically, so two logins at the same moment cannot both succeed.

DECISION 3

Multi-zone storage

Sessions live in multi-zone Aerospike, so the service keeps running if one zone has a problem.

DECISION 4

Reusable validation

A shared client SDK lets the playback and SMS services validate tokens the same way, so there is one place to change the rules.

Failure handling and recovery

A zone has a problem

The other zones keep serving, so sessions stay available.

The set-top-box details call fails

Login still works.

Two logins at once

The atomic limit lets only one succeed.

Results

IPTV login and device-level playback in one service that scales with traffic and tolerates a zone failure.
