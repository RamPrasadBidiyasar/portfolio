05 · PhilosophyThree habits the case studies share

How I think about systems.

I.

Design for ordering

Decide what must happen in order, then make the system enforce it, for example by keying Kafka messages by user.

Seen in · 01 · 04

II.

Isolate failure

Give products, thread pools and zones their own boundaries, and keep a degraded path that still answers.

Seen in · 03 · 05

III.

Measure before and after

Pick the metric first, take the baseline, and check it again after rollout.

Seen in · all five
